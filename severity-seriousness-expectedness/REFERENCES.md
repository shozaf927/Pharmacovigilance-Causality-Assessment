# References — Primary Regulatory and Pharmacovigilance Sources

Accessed/reviewed for this educational project: 12 November 2026. Always verify the latest version before operational use.

## 1. ICH E2A — Clinical Safety Data Management
International Council for Harmonisation. *E2A: Clinical Safety Data Management: Definitions and Standards for Expedited Reporting.* 1994/1995 guideline.
- Official PDF: https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E2A_Guideline.pdf
- Supports: AE/ADR terminology, seriousness criteria, medically important events, distinction between severity and seriousness, expectedness and clinical-development expedited reporting.

## 2. ICH E2D(R1) — Post-Approval Safety Data
International Council for Harmonisation. *E2D(R1): Post-Approval Safety Data: Definitions and Standards for Management and Reporting of Individual Case Safety Reports.* Final Step 4 version adopted 15 September 2025.
- Official ICH PDF: https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/ICH_E2D%28R1%29_Step4_FinalGuideline_2025_0819.pdf
- EMA guideline page and EU implementation information: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/scientific-guidelines/ich-e2d-post-approval-safety-data-management
- Supports: contemporary post-authorisation ICSR definitions and management/reporting standards. EU legal effective date stated by EMA: 18 March 2026.

## 3. Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission — PvPI FAQ and Toolkit
Pharmacovigilance Programme of India, Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission.
- PvPI FAQ: https://ipc.gov.in/PvPI/faq.html
- PvPI toolkit: https://ipc.gov.in/PvPI/toolkit.html
- Supports: Indian serious-event criteria, encouragement to report suspected ADRs irrespective of known/unknown or serious/non-serious status, and PvPI pharmacovigilance resources.

## 4. IPC — Guidance Document for Marketing Authorisation Holders
Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission. *Pharmacovigilance Guidance Document for Marketing Authorisation Holders of Pharmaceutical Products* (2024 edition).
- Official PDF: https://www.ipc.gov.in/images/Guidance_Document_-_17.9.2024_5_mb.pdf
- Supports: MAH reporting procedures and specified India reporting timelines (15 calendar days for serious AEs and 90 calendar days for non-serious AEs, as described in the document). Check current applicability and amendments.

## 5. EMA — Good Pharmacovigilance Practices
European Medicines Agency. *Guidelines on Good Pharmacovigilance Practices (GVP)*.
- Official overview and modules: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/post-authorisation/pharmacovigilance-post-authorisation/good-pharmacovigilance-practices-gvp
- Module VI concerns collection, management and submission of suspected adverse reaction reports. EMA notes that ICH E2D(R1) implementation and regional updates should be considered.

## 6. FDA — ICH E2D(R1) final guidance
U.S. Food and Drug Administration. *E2D(R1) Post-Approval Safety Data: Definitions and Standards for Management and Reporting of Individual Case Safety Reports.* March 2026.
- Official guidance page: https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/e2dr1-post-approval-safety-data-definitions-and-standards-management-and-reporting-individual-case
- Supports: U.S. publication/availability of final ICH E2D(R1) guidance.

## 7. Scope and interpretation
- This project is an educational summary. Primary regulatory texts take precedence over summaries.
- The project does not reproduce complete copyrighted guidance documents.
- Local implementation, product category, clinical-trial status and company SOP may change the applicable requirements.
- Fictional RSI excerpts in case studies are not real labels and must never be cited as product-specific evidence.
