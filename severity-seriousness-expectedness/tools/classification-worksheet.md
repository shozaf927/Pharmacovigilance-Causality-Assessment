## Quality checks
- [ ] Severity is not used as a proxy for seriousness.
- [ ] Every serious classification identifies at least one criterion.
- [ ] Life-threatening means actual risk at the time, not hypothetical potential.
- [ ] Expectedness is supported by the correct RSI version.
- [ ] Causality is separate.
- [ ] Missing data and follow-up are documented.
- [ ] Reporting timeline is jurisdiction- and pathway-specific.
- [ ] No patient-identifiable information is included in portfolio examples.
