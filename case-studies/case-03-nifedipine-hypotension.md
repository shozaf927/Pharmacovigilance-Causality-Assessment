# Case 03: Nifedipine-Associated Hypotension (Fictional)

## Case narrative
A fictional patient experiences dizziness and low blood pressure after starting or increasing nifedipine. The vignette does not specify the dose, formulation, blood pressure values, timing, hydration status, comorbidities or concomitant antihypertensive medicines.

## Evidence review
- Suspected drug: nifedipine.
- Event: hypotension with dizziness.
- Pharmacological plausibility: peripheral vasodilation can lower blood pressure.
- Timing: reported after initiation or dose increase, but exact interval is unavailable.
- Alternative causes: dehydration, infection, cardiac causes and other blood-pressure-lowering medicines need review.
- Dechallenge: unknown.
- Rechallenge: not required and should not be performed solely for scoring.

## Information needed

- Concomitant medicines and recent changes.

## Learning point
